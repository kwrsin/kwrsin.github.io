ACROSS RIDER

Synopsis

The protagonist decides to earn prize money from motorbike races to make his girlfriend happy. However, naive and inexperienced, he signs a contract with a shady agent and ends up entering races. What will the outcome be...?

Rules

Finish each stage within the time limit and place in the top 3 to succeed.

Controls & Interface

Title Screen

Use the left and right cursor buttons to choose between EASY and HARD modes, and press the confirm button to start the game.

Game Screen

Status

① Coins: Number of coins
② Time: Countdown timer
③ Fuel: Remaining fuel
④ Karma: Difficulty increases
⑤ Jewels: A happy ending?
⑥ Attack: Consumes one per battle
⑦ Guard: Consumes one per battle

Controller

  • Cursor Up / Down: Move the player up and down.
  • Confirm Button: Move forward while accelerating.

Actions

  • Attack: Ram into enemies to battle.
  • Guard: Automatically triggers attack evasion during battles.

Characters

  • Protagonist: The player character.
  • Agent: Supports race entries (a shady agent).
  • Girlfriend: The protagonist's girlfriend.
  • The Chutars: Rival racers.

Stages

  • Beginner Course: Easy course for Beginners.
  • God wind: Watch out for sudden gusts of wind!?
  • Dirty Party: An orderly-down-town?
  • Death Trains: Safety first.
  • Holy Power: Be respectful!
