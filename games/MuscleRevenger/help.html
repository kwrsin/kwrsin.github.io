MUSCLE REVENGER - Game Manual

Story

Match, a justice-driven professional wrestler, is suddenly attacked by a mysterious figure while training with his teammates in a gym. After a fierce battle, he loses consciousness. When he awakens to the sound of sirens, all of his teammates have been killed. Police arrive and, for some reason, arrest Match as the suspect, taking away his trademark mask.

Despite insisting on his innocence, Match is imprisoned. He soon realizes the incident was a setup. Behind it all is a ruthless prison warden who fabricated the case for his own promotion. Discovering that his stolen mask holds a hidden secret, Match decides to escape prison, avenge his friends, and prove his innocence.

Game Rules

In each stage, avoid interference from guards within the time limit, collect the key, and reach the exit to clear the stage.

Controls

  • Cursor [PC KEYBORD]: Move the player up, down, left, and right
  • Item Button [PC KEYBORD => b]: Use collected items
  • Jump Button [PC KEYBORD => space]: Make the player jump & game start

Characters

  • Player (Match)
  • Guard
  • Guard Boss

Items

  • Protein A: Restores health
  • Protein B: Increases speed
  • Time Extension: Adds time to the countdown timer
